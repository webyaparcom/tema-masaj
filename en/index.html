Sessions

Let’s choose the right massage together

Every session starts with a short pre-talk; pressure, duration and focus area are adjusted to you.

  1. 01

    Classic Swedish massage

    Full body with long, rhythmic strokes; 50 or 80 minutes.

  2. 02

    Deep-tissue massage

    Firm pressure on back, neck and shoulders; if you have pain or a condition, consult your doctor first.

  3. 03

    Aromatherapy massage

    A gently paced session with the plant oil you choose; mention any allergy when booking.

  4. 04

    Foot reflexology

    A 40-minute relaxation session focused on the soles of the feet.

  5. 05

    Hot stone massage

    Muscle relaxation with warm basalt stones; not offered during pregnancy.

  6. 06

    Couples room

    Simultaneous sessions with two therapists in one room; weekdays recommended.
